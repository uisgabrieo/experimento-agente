**Invariants** — INV1/2/3/4/5/7 are checked by one function (`validar_escala`) over the *proposed final set* of a professional's confirmed shifts, so allocation (RF13) and swap approval (RF25) share identical semantics; the swap validates both professionals before any write, making RF25 atomic by construction. INV6 is held by `normalizar_fila`/`convocar_se_necessario`; INV8 is recomputed after every create/cancel/swap and on `carga_semanal` change. Minimum coverage is treated as an operational goal, not an invariant (4.2).

**Judgment calls worth your review:**
- `GET /turnos/{id}/substituicoes` (RF36) returns a bare JSON array — the spec says `{itens, total}` explicitly elsewhere but not here.
- `horas_periodo` (RF23) sums the *full* durations of the returned shifts rather than clipping to `[de, ate)`; the two differ only for shifts partially overlapping the window.
- `POST /profissionais/{id}/especialidades` (RF06) returns the updated professional plus `profissional_id`/`especialidade_id`, since the spec doesn't name a shape for it.
- References to non-existent FKs (e.g. unknown `turno_id` on `POST /plantoes`) return 404, not 409.
